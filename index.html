    OUR STORY

    Prime Food Marketing is a Singapore based food distributor with the heart for expanding specialised products from the Philippines. Hinting from our company title, you will find line items that are meticulously sourced with an innovative selection of premium quality products in mind. 

     

    Singapore : The food metropolis reputable for it's myriad of culturally diverse cuisines is nothing short of amazing. Singaporeans find excitement in innovative flavours and it's selection of taste profiles keeps expanding. We believe there is much to explore and love with Philippine cuisine; so we want to bring that to you! Because as Anthony Bourdain predicts, "Filipino cuisine will be the next big food trend"!
